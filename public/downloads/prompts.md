### Define Your Goal in Writing

Turn a vague wish into one specific written goal — what, why, and what success looks like.

Fill in: [GOAL AREA], [BROAD GOAL], [TIMEFRAME]

Help me define my biggest goal clearly, in writing. My goal area is [GOAL AREA], and what I want in broad terms is [BROAD GOAL]. Ask me up to 5 short clarifying questions, one at a time if needed, then help me write it as one specific sentence: what I will achieve, by [TIMEFRAME], and how I will know it is done. Then add two lines: why it matters to me, and what my life looks like on a normal day once it is achieved. Keep it concrete and measurable where possible. Do not promise that writing it down guarantees the outcome.

### Visualize Your Future Life

### FTBA Alignment Check
